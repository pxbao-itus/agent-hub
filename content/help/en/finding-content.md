## Search before you create

Agent Hub is most useful when a team reuses maintained content instead of
creating near-duplicates. Start with the global search when you know what you
need, or browse a specific artifact type when you know its format.

Search can combine keyword matching with semantic similarity when embeddings
are enabled by the instance operator. If semantic search is unavailable, Agent
Hub continues with keyword search. Use clear domain terms and try a shorter
query when results are too narrow.

## Narrow the results

Use kind and tag filters to reduce unrelated results. Tags work best when they
describe stable domains, systems, or purposes rather than temporary status.

**Explore** is intended for public artifacts that may be useful outside their
owner group. Being public affects discovery, not edit permission.

## Reuse an artifact

After opening a result:

1. Check its owner, visibility, current version, and recent update time.
2. Read its body and related artifacts to confirm it fits the task.
3. Favorite it when you expect to return to it.
4. Use it directly through MCP, add it to a Project or Workflow, or create
   a duplicate when you need an independently maintained copy.

A duplicate starts its own version history. Some references may be preserved or
skipped depending on your access.

## When nothing appears

- Remove filters and retry with fewer words.
- Search for the concept rather than an exact title.
- Confirm that you belong to the expected owner space.
- Ask the owner to share the artifact if it is outside your group access.
- Remember that assignment alone does not make an artifact visible.

See [Reuse, import, and export](/help/reuse-and-portability) before copying
content between spaces or Agent Hub instances.
